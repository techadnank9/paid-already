# Paid Already: 3-minute demo

**Open:** https://paid-already.lovable.app

## 0:00 Hook (20s)
"Since July, 3,956 TikTok and Instagram videos say 'this is not sponsored.' Creators give brands millions in free exposure, and the brands can't even see it, because 76% of it is never tagged. Paid Already finds it and turns it into a deal."

## 0:20 Scan (40s)
- Click **Try: @mikaylanogueira** (17.4M followers).
- "We watched her last 40 videos with Oriane: every word spoken, every bit of on-screen text, every frame."
- Point at **$710,910** of free promotion to **18 brands**, **71% hidden** (never tagged).
- Point at **4 brands already paying creators right now** (Oriane search across the whole index).

## 1:00 Proof pack (50s)
- Scroll to **Maybelline**: "1,211 sponsored posts in 90 days. They pay creators. They just don't pay her."
- Click the evidence tile, and **the video plays right here**.
- Show the moments timeline: "Said it at 1:13: '…a foundation that photographs really well is the Maybelline Superstay…'"
- On **Bro Denim**, show the on-screen frames with the brand **boxed in lime** (Oriane on-screen text detection).

## 1:50 Sponsor reel (30s)
- Play the **sponsor reel**: an auto-cut 9:16 video of every moment the brand appears, in the brand's own colors and logo (scraped with Firecrawl), ending with her stats and business email.
- "This is what she sends the brand. Proof in 15 seconds."

## 2:20 Pitch (30s)
- Click **Write my pitch to Maybelline** (GMI Cloud model): a real email with her stats, the exact quote, and a link to her shareable **proof page**.
- Open **View proof page**: a brand-facing media kit at /proof/mikaylanogueira/Maybelline.

## 2:50 Close (10s)
"Every creator has unpaid brand deals sitting in their camera roll. Paid Already finds them, proves them, and pitches them. Monday morning, agencies run this across all 50 creators."

**Stack:** Oriane (transcripts, on-screen text, frames, sponsor search) · GMI Cloud (brand extraction and pitches) · Lovable (site) · Firecrawl (brand logos and colors) · Render (API)
